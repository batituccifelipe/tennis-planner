💰 Money-Saving Strategies
  • Book 4-6 months ahead: Save 40-60% on flights
  • Use Airbnb: 2-3 bed apartments (€1,200-1,800/week) vs. 2 hotel rooms (€2,400-3,600)
  • Return flight on finals day: Prices drop 20-30% as event ends
  • Grocery shop locally: Cook breakfasts + pack lunches (save €200-300/week)
  • Kids tickets: Children under 12 often get 50% off; kids 5 may be free
  • Travel insurance once/year: €150-200 covers all trips
  • Avoid official partner hotels: Book independently, save 40%
  • Use cash-back cards: 3-5% on flights = €150-300 per trip
  • Rail pass for Europe: Eurail cheaper than flights between European tournaments
  • Group booking: Partner with other families for 10-15% hotel discount
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Travel (Ages 5 & 8)
  • Accommodation: Book suites or 2-bed apartments (kids need separate sleeping space)
  • Match timing: Skip first round; jump in for quarterfinals (better tennis + kids less bored)
  • Bring entertainment: Portable games, headphones, snacks (matches = 3+ hours)
  • Kids' clubs: Many tournaments offer kids' tennis clinics (€30-50, highly recommended)
  • Meal budget: €40-60/day in Portugal/Spain, €60-100 in UK/France
  • School absences: Max 2 weeks/year - align with holidays when possible
  • Jet lag: Arrive 3-4 days early for long-haul (Australia, US)
  • Check vaccines 6 weeks before: Yellow fever required for some destinations
  • Pool/kids club hotels: Invest slightly more for breaks during tournament days
  • Flight timing: Avoid red-eyes with young kids; window seats for parents, aisle for bathroom access
